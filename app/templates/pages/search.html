    What is hybrid search?

    Meaning

    Every passage is turned into a vector on the CPU. Passages that say the same thing in other words rank high.

    Keywords

    BM25 full-text search finds the exact terms: names, functions, error messages.

    Fused

    Reciprocal Rank Fusion merges both rankings. A passage that is high in both comes first: that's the hit.

    All of it is one query against RAGite's docs_chunks table: WHERE docs_chunks MATCH 'your question'. Every search links to its SQL.
